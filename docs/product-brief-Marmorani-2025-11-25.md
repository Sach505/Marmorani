# Product Brief: Marmorani

**Date:** 2025-11-25
**Author:** Sacha
**Context:** e-commerce startup

---

## Executive Summary

Marmorani is an e-commerce platform that enables customers (both individuals and interior design professionals) to create custom marble cubes for home decoration. Born from the founder's personal desire for a custom marble cube that wasn't available on the market, the platform addresses the gap between standard decorative marble products and truly personalized, made-to-order pieces. The solution combines the founder's expertise in marble production, fabrication, and resale with a design-focused, simple-to-use online experience.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

The market for decorative marble cubes offers only standard sizes and designs through various home decor websites, with no option for custom-made pieces. This gap exists for both true marble and faux marble products. Customers who want a specific size, finish, or design to fit their exact space and aesthetic vision have no accessible solution. This limitation affects both individual consumers seeking unique home decor and interior design professionals who need custom pieces for their clients' projects.

### Problem Impact

The lack of accessible custom marble cube solutions forces customers into two unsatisfactory paths:
- **Standard products**: Compromise on size, design, or quality to fit available options
- **Direct manufacturer contact**: Face complex, time-consuming processes with high prices that make custom pieces unaffordable for most consumers

This creates a significant gap in the market where demand for personalized, high-quality marble decor exists but remains unserved.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

**No specialized platforms exist** for custom marble cube creation. While generic home decor sites offer standard marble products, they don't provide customization capabilities.

**Direct manufacturer contact** is the only alternative, but it presents significant barriers:
- **Complexity**: Requires multiple interactions, quotes, negotiations, and coordination that most consumers find overwhelming
- **High cost**: Pricing structures for one-off custom orders are typically prohibitive, making custom marble cubes inaccessible to the average consumer
- **Lack of transparency**: No clear pricing, design visualization, or standardized process
- **Time-consuming**: Lengthy back-and-forth communication without a streamlined interface

### Proposed Solution

Marmorani is an e-commerce platform that allows customers to design and order custom marble cubes online through a simple, design-focused experience. The platform serves both individual consumers and interior design professionals with the same streamlined interface, recognizing that both segments value simplicity and quality.

**Core Platform Capabilities:**
- **Visual configurator**: Interactive tool allowing customers to select dimensions (height, width, depth), marble type, and finish options
- **Automatic weight calculation**: Real-time weight calculation based on dimensions and marble density, essential for shipping logistics and customer information
- **Transparent pricing**: Automatic, real-time pricing calculation based on selections, eliminating the need for quotes and negotiations
- **3D visualization**: Customers can see their custom cube in 3D before ordering, ensuring the design matches their vision
- **Inspiration gallery**: Curated collection showcasing different marble types, finishes, and use cases to inspire customers
- **Streamlined ordering**: Simple checkout process that transforms custom marble ordering from a complex, multi-step process into a few clicks
- **Quality standard**: All cubes are delivered with protective pads (patins) included, ensuring cubes never sit directly on the floor and protecting both the cube and flooring surfaces

**Competitive Advantage:**
The platform leverages the founder's direct ownership of both the manufacturing facility and marble supply, enabling competitive pricing that traditional manufacturers cannot match. This vertical integration eliminates middlemen and allows for optimized production processes.

### Key Differentiators

**Specialized focus**: Marmorani is the first platform dedicated specifically to custom marble cube creation, not a general decor site with limited customization.

**Accessibility through simplicity**: Transforms the complex, intimidating process of ordering custom marble into an intuitive, design-focused online experience.

**Competitive pricing**: Leverages the founder's direct expertise in marble production, fabrication, and resale to offer custom pieces at accessible price points that traditional manufacturers cannot match.

**Design-first experience**: Platform prioritizes visual design and simplicity, making the customization process enjoyable rather than transactional.

**Unified experience**: Serves both individual consumers and professionals with the same simple, design-focused interface, recognizing that both segments value ease of use, quality, and customization equally.

**Individual Consumers:**
- Homeowners and design enthusiasts who want custom marble cubes for their living spaces
- Use cases: coffee tables, side tables, decorative columns, end-of-sofa pieces
- Value: Personalization, unique design, quality materials
- Technical comfort: Varies, but platform must be simple enough for non-technical users

**Interior Design Professionals:**
- Architects and interior designers who need custom marble pieces for client projects
- Use cases: Bespoke furniture pieces, architectural elements, custom installations
- Value: Professional-grade customization, project management capabilities, quality assurance
- Technical comfort: Generally higher, but simplicity remains important for efficiency

### User Journey

**Discovery Phase:**
- Customers discover Marmorani through multiple channels: organic search (SEO), paid advertising, social media marketing, and word-of-mouth referrals
- Initial landing experience presents both inspiration gallery and direct access to the configurator, allowing users to choose their preferred entry point

- High-detail 3D visualization with accurate marble texture helps customers feel confident in their design choices
- Simple checkout process with secure payment and automatic invoice generation

- Email notifications keep customers informed throughout production and delivery

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

**1. Custom Cube Configurator**
- Interactive dimension selector (height, width, depth) with real-time validation
- Marble type selection from 10 available marble types at launch, each with high-quality product images
- Finish options (polished, honed, raw, etc.) with visual previews
- **Automatic weight calculation**: Real-time weight calculation based on dimensions and marble type density (required feature)
- Real-time automatic pricing calculation based on dimensions, marble type, and finish
- Pricing structure to be defined during development phase

**2. 3D Visualization**
- High-detail 3D rendering of the custom cube with accurate marble texture representation
- Real-time updates as customers modify dimensions, marble type, or finish
- Interactive viewing (rotate, zoom) to examine the cube from all angles
- Texture mapping that accurately represents the selected marble type's unique characteristics

**3. E-commerce Core**
- Shopping cart functionality
- Secure checkout process with payment gateway integration
- International delivery address management and validation
- Delivery lead time: 4 weeks from order to delivery
- **Standard inclusion**: All cubes are delivered with protective pads/feet (patins) to prevent direct contact with the floor - this is automatically included with every order
- Automatic invoice generation upon order completion
- Order confirmation emails with invoice attachment

**4. Content & Discovery**
- Inspiration gallery showcasing marble types, finishes, and real-world use cases
- Product detail pages for each marble type with specifications, images, and characteristics
- Use case examples (coffee tables, side tables, columns, etc.) to inspire customers

**5. Order Management**
- Customer dashboard for order tracking
- Order status updates (pending, in production, shipped, delivered)
- Email notifications for order status changes
- Order history and invoice access

### Out of Scope for MVP

- Additional customization options such as base/feet options, backlighting/retroéclairage, and other enhancements (planned for future releases)
- Professional account features (same experience for all users in MVP)
- Sample ordering system
- Bulk ordering capabilities

### MVP Success Criteria

- Platform successfully processes custom marble cube orders end-to-end
- 3D visualization accurately represents marble textures and helps customers make confident purchase decisions
- Transparent pricing and simple checkout process eliminate friction in custom marble ordering
- International delivery capability operational
- Customer dashboard provides effective order tracking
- Initial target: 2 orders per month to validate product-market fit

### Future Vision

**Enhanced Customization Options:**
- Base/feet options for different cube applications
- Backlighting/retroéclairage integration for decorative cubes
- Additional customization features based on customer feedback and demand

**Platform Enhancements:**
- Professional account features for interior designers and architects
- Sample ordering system to help customers choose marble types
- Bulk ordering capabilities for professional users
- Advanced project management tools for design professionals

## Risks and Assumptions

**Key Assumptions:**
- Market demand exists for custom marble cubes at accessible price points
- Customers value the simplicity and transparency of online customization over traditional manufacturer processes
- 3D visualization will provide sufficient confidence for customers to purchase without physical samples
- International shipping logistics can be managed effectively for marble products
- Production capacity can scale with demand

**Risks to Monitor:**
- Pricing strategy needs validation to ensure competitive positioning while maintaining profitability
- Technical complexity of high-detail 3D rendering with accurate marble textures
- International shipping costs and logistics for heavy marble products
- Customer acquisition and conversion rates in a new market category
- Production lead times and capacity management as order volume grows
